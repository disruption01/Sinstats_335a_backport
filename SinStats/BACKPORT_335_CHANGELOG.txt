SinStats 5.902-335.0.6
- Fixed Settings page construction on WoW 3.3.5a by not registering unsupported OnHyperlinkEnter/OnHyperlinkLeave scripts on generic Separator frames.
- Those hyperlink handlers are cosmetic only; 3.3.5a rejects them at SetScript(), which previously aborted the Settings -> HUD page before its controls were created.
- Expected result: the main Settings/HUD controls can now finish rendering instead of leaving the page blank.
